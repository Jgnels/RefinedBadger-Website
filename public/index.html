Copperlight

From first hearth to the stars.

Copperlight begins close to the ground: water, food, shelter, relationships, and a fire beneath a dangerous, beautiful sky. Nearby, old stones answer the aurora.

As generations pass, households become clans and settlements, settlements become realms and civilizations, and the player's perspective grows with them without erasing the people and places that came before.

Person / Hearth→ Clan→ Settlement→ Realm→ Civilization→ Stars

People remain people.

History leaves matter behind.

The world remembers.
