Current development

Real projects. Real builds.

RefinedBadger Studios LLC is an independent game studio in Utah. The images below are captured from projects currently in development.

Our slate spans gothic narrative, restoration and survival, life simulation, strategy, horror, and historical adventure.

Gothic narrative · In development

Carmilla

A gothic narrative game inspired by Sheridan Le Fanu's novella, built around memory, relationships, routine, atmosphere, and a living castle.

First-person restoration · In development

Island Restoration

Restore a remote lighthouse island, explore its buildings and coast, travel by boat, fish, dive wrecks, and uncover why the island was abandoned.

Cat life simulation · In development

Purrborough

A village cat simulation about household routines, exploration, needs, social bonds, and a world designed to be experienced at cat height.

Historical adventure · In development

Viking

A grounded Viking settlement and adventure project set on a rugged coast, with everyday life, people, animals, and working spaces sharing the same world.

The studio

Small team. Reusable technology. Multiple worlds.

RefinedBadger develops games alongside reusable studio systems for simulation, interaction, persistence, AI, UI, weather, combat, and production automation.

That shared foundation lets each project spend more of its effort on the world, tone, and player experience instead of rebuilding the same infrastructure from scratch.
