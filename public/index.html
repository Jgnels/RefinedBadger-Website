Current development

Different worlds. Shared craft.

RefinedBadger Studios LLC is an independent game studio in Utah developing atmospheric narrative, restoration, simulation, strategy, and horror projects.

Our games share reusable studio technology while keeping distinct settings, mechanics, and visual identities.

Gothic narrative · In development

Marcilla

A gothic narrative game inspired by Sheridan Le Fanu's Carmilla, built around memory, relationships, routine, atmosphere, and a living castle.

First-person restoration · In development

Keeper's Reach

Cat life simulation · In development

Willow Mews

A village cat simulation about household routines, exploration, needs, social bonds, and seeing a colorful town from a cat's point of view.

The studio

Reusable technology. Multiple worlds.

RefinedBadger develops games alongside reusable studio systems for simulation, interaction, persistence, AI, UI, weather, combat, and production automation.

That shared foundation lets each project spend more effort on its world, tone, and player experience instead of rebuilding the same infrastructure from scratch.
